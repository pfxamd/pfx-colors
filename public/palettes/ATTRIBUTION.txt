PFx COLORS / EXPLORE — SOURCE CREDITS AND LICENSES

These palettes are bundled locally for browsing. Names and hex values remain attributed
to their respective source projects. A color value itself does not imply ownership.

CSS Named Colors — W3C CSS Color Module Level 4
https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/#named-colors
148 CSS named entries (including standard aliases).

Color Names — meodai/color-names — MIT
https://github.com/meodai/color-names
Source: src/colornames.csv (GitHub main snapshot 2026-10-18).

Open Color — yeun/open-color — MIT
https://github.com/yeun/open-color
Source: open-color.json.

Radix Colors — radix-ui/colors — MIT
https://github.com/radix-ui/colors
Source: src/light.ts and src/dark.ts, opaque sRGB HEX scales only (no alpha or Display-P3).

Tailwind Colors — tailwindlabs/tailwindcss — MIT
https://github.com/tailwindlabs/tailwindcss
Source: tag v3.4.17, src/public/colors.js, HEX swatches.

D3 Scale Chromatic — d3/d3-scale-chromatic — ISC + ColorBrewer Apache-2.0
https://github.com/d3/d3-scale-chromatic
Source: categorical, diverging and sequential multi-color discrete schemes.
